Pythagorean vs. Chaldean Numerology

While modern western practices largely rely on the Pythagorean system, Vedic Numerology often aligns closely with the ancient Chaldean system. Understanding the distinction is crucial for an accurate reading.

The Pythagorean system assigns numbers 1 through 9 sequentially to the alphabet (A=1, B=2... I=9, J=1 etc.). It is highly structural and focuses on sequential order.

Conversely, the Chaldean system, originating in ancient Babylon, is based on the vibrational essence and sound of letters rather than sequential order. It only uses numbers 1 through 8 (reserving 9 as a sacred number kept apart from standard letter assignment). This system is considered older, more complex, and often more precise for determining inner realities and hidden karmic patterns.
