The Science of Your Soul's Journey

A Kundli, also known as a Janam Patrika or Birth Chart, is a deeply personalized map of the heavens at the precise moment of your birth. In Vedic Astrology (Jyotish), this chart serves as the foundational blueprint of your life. It is not merely a fortune-telling tool, but a profound psychological and spiritual guide.

The chart maps the twelve houses, each representing different facets of your human experience—from early childhood and wealth, to partnerships, career, and spiritual liberation. By calculating the exact degrees of the Ascendant (Lagna) and the nine planetary bodies, a Kundli reveals the karmic patterns you are destined to navigate.

Why Precision Matters

Even a difference of a few minutes in birth time can alter the Ascendant or shift a planet into a different Navamsha (divisional chart), completely changing the interpretation of your destiny. Accuracy is paramount for an authentic reading.

Reading the Basic Chart

While profound interpretation requires years of study, the basic structure of the North Indian style Kundli is accessible to beginners. The central top diamond represents the First House, or the Ascendant, dictating your core personality and physical vessel.
