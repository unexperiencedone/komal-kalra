SEC. 03

Cancellation Window

48-Hour Notice Required

To be eligible for a full refund on scheduled consultations, you must submit a cancellation request at least 48 hours prior to the appointment time.

Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours before the scheduled appointment will be eligible for a 50% refund, or the option to reschedule without penalty. Cancellations made less than 24 hours prior to the appointment, or missed appointments (no-shows), are non-refundable.

Approved refunds will be processed within 5 to 7 business days from the date of approval. The funds will be returned to the original payment method used during the transaction.
