The 36-Point Guna Milan System

In Vedic Astrology, Kundli Matching (or Guna Milan) is the foundational step before consecrating a marriage. It is based on the position of the Moon in the natal charts of the prospective bride and groom. The system evaluates 8 categories (Ashtakoot), each carrying a specific weightage, culminating in a total of 36 points (Gunas).

A minimum score of 18 out of 36 is traditionally considered acceptable for marriage. However, a higher score denotes greater alignment in aspects such as mental compatibility, financial stability, physical attraction, and spiritual synergy.

Understanding Mangal Dosha

Beyond the Guna Milan, analyzing Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) is critical. If Mars is placed in specific houses (1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th) in the natal chart, it creates an aggressive energy that can cause friction in marital life. A thorough matching process assesses the presence of this Dosha and suggests profound remedies to neutralize its effects.

Beyond the Score: A Holistic View

While the 36-point system provides a structural baseline, true astrological compatibility requires analyzing the strength of the 7th House (the house of marriage), the position of Venus (significator of love), and Jupiter (significator of wisdom and expansion). A high Guna score does not guarantee bliss if fundamental planetary alignments clash.
